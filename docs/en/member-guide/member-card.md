# Digital member card

Your digital member card with a QR code, to identify yourself at access control.

> **In Spanish:** [Carné digital](../../es/member-guide/member-card.md).

## Your card

In **My Card** you will find your **digital member card**, with your **member number**, your
**status** and a **QR code**.

- **Download PDF**, to save it on your phone or print it.
- At **access control**, show the QR code so staff can check your status.

If your card is not available, your organisation may not have enabled member cards
yet; contact them.

## Profile photo

You can add a **profile photo** (JPG, PNG or WebP) from your profile, which will be shown on your
card and in the application.

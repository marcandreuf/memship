# Your account and profile

How to access the member portal and manage your details.

> **In Spanish:** [Tu cuenta y tu perfil](../../es/member-guide/account.md).

## Signing in

Sign in with your **email** and your **password**.

### Forgot your password?

Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen, enter your email and you will receive a
link to reset it. Open the link, type your **new password** and sign in
again. Reset links **expire**; if yours is no longer valid,
request a new one.

## Your profile

From your profile you can:

- View and update your **personal** and **contact** details.
- Change the interface **language** (Spanish, Catalan or English).
- Upload or change your **profile photo** (JPG, PNG or WebP).

### Custom fields

Your organisation can define its own **profile fields** (for example, kit size or
licence number). Depending on how they are set up, you will be able to edit some and others will be
**read-only**.

## What you will find in the portal

- Your **[activities](activities.md)** and the registrations open to you.
- Your **[receipts and payments](payments.md)**, and your **payment method**.
- Your **[digital member card](member-card.md)** with a QR code.
- The organisation's **announcements** and your **notifications**.

# Payments and receipts

How to view your receipts, pay them online and choose your payment method.

> **In Spanish:** [Pagos y recibos](../../es/member-guide/payments.md).

## My Receipts

In **My Receipts** you see your receipts with their **status** (Pending, Issued, Paid, Overdue…),
the **amount** and the dates. You can **download the PDF** of each receipt.

## Paying online

If your organisation has online payment enabled, pending receipts show the
**Pay Now** button, with these options depending on the configuration:

- **Card (Stripe)**.
- **Card (Redsys)**, with 3D Secure verification.
- **Bizum** (through Redsys).

After paying you will see a confirmation. The **final confirmation** may take a few moments,
because it comes from the payment provider; if it stays in progress, you will receive an email
when it is confirmed. If a payment does not complete, you can **try again**.

## Your payment method

In **Payment Method** you choose how you prefer to pay your fees:

- **Direct Debit (SEPA)**: an automatic charge to your account. Requires an **active SEPA
  mandate**.
- **Bank Transfer**: a manual transfer to the organisation's account.
- **Cash**: payment in person.
- **Card**.

### Direct debit (SEPA)

For direct debit you must enter your **bank details**: **IBAN**, **BIC/SWIFT** (usually
detected from the IBAN) and the **account holder**. The section shows the status of your
**SEPA mandate** (Active, Cancelled, Expired or No mandate). If you do not have an active mandate,
**contact the organisation** to sign one.

# Activities

How to register for your organisation's activities and manage your registrations.

> **In Spanish:** [Actividades](../../es/member-guide/activities.md).
> **En català:** [Activitats](../../ca/member-guide/activities.md).

## Browsing and registering

In the **activities** section you will see the published activities, with their image, dates,
available places and registration period. To register:

1. Open the activity and press **Register**.
2. If the activity has **modalities** or several **prices**, select the one that applies.
3. Accept the required **consents** and attach the required **documents**, if there
   are any.
4. Enter a **discount code** if you have one.
5. Confirm the registration.

You can only register if you meet the activity's **eligibility rules** (membership
type, age, status). If you do not meet one of them, the activity will say so.

## Waitlist

If the activity is **full**, your registration goes onto the **waitlist**. When a place is
freed up, waitlisted registrations are **promoted automatically** in order, and
you will receive a notice.

## My Activities

In **My Activities** you see your registrations and their status: **Confirmed**, **Waitlisted**,
**Pending** or **Cancelled**.

## Cancelling a registration

If the activity allows **cancellation by the member**, you can cancel your registration up to the
set deadline (a number of hours before the start). After that deadline, contact the
organisation.

Registering may generate a **[receipt](payments.md)**; if you cancel within the deadline, the
associated receipt is voided.

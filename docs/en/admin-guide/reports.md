# Reports and dashboard

Dashboard, financial overview, annual summary and data exports.

> **In Spanish:** [Informes y panel](../../es/admin-guide/reports.md).
> **En català:** [Informes i tauler](../../ca/admin-guide/reports.md).

## Dashboard

The administrator's dashboard summarises the state of the organisation:

- **Members**, active, pending and total.
- **Activities**, published, draft and total; and **registrations** by status
  (confirmed, waitlisted, cancelled, pending).
- **Finance overview**, **pending** amount, **paid this month** and **overdue**, with
  receipt status charts.
- **Next recurring billing**, the day and the days remaining, if it is enabled.

## Annual summary

From the dashboard, **Annual summary** gives the financial view of the financial year (income
and outstanding by year), useful for the year-end close and the annual report.

## Exports

Memship lets you **export data to CSV** for use in spreadsheets or in the organisation's
accounting.

> As new reports and export formats are added, they will be documented on this
> page.

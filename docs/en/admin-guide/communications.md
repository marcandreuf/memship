# Communications

Sending announcements to members by email and as an in-app notification.

> **In Spanish:** [Comunicaciones](../../es/admin-guide/communications.md).

> **Prerequisite.** Enable communications in
> [Settings → Members → Communications](settings.md#emails-to-members) and set up
> [email delivery](../self-hosting/email.md).

## Creating an announcement

From **Communications → New announcement**:

1. **Content**: a **subject** and the **message**. The body supports **basic Markdown**
   (bold, italics, links and lists) and has a **preview**.
2. **Audience**: choose who it is sent to:
   - **All members**.
   - A specific **group**.
   - A **membership type**.

   Selecting the audience shows how many members will receive it.
3. **Save draft** to finish it later, or **Send**.

## Sending

On sending, the announcement is delivered **by email** and as an **in-app notification** to the
selected audience.

> **A sent announcement cannot be edited.** Check it carefully before confirming the send.

## Tracking

On the page of a sent announcement, the **Recipients** tab shows, per person, the
**channel** (email / in-app) and whether they have **seen** it. The summary shows the send date,
the number of recipients and how many have seen it.

Members see announcements in their portal and receive a **notification**; they can mark them as
read.

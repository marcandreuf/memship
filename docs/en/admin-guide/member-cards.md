# Digital member card and access control

A digital member card with a QR code, verified at access control.

> **In Spanish:** [Carné digital y control de acceso](../../es/admin-guide/member-cards.md).

> **Prerequisite.** Enable member cards in
> [Settings → Members → Member Card](settings.md#other-options).

## Member numbering

In the card settings you configure the **numbering**:

- **Number prefix**, e.g. `SCB-` generates `SCB-0001`.
- **Digits**: the length of the zero-padded sequence.
- **Assign numbers** to members who do not have one yet; it shows how many numbers were
  assigned.

## The member's card

Every member has a **digital member card** in their portal, with their member number, their
status and a **QR code**, which they can **download as a PDF**. See the
[member guide → Digital member card](../member-guide/member-card.md).

## Access control (scanning)

The **Scan Member Card** page lets you check a member's status at access
control:

- **Scan with camera** the QR code on the card, or **Enter code manually**.
- The result shows the member **number** and their **status** (Active, Pending, Suspended,
  Cancelled or Expired).
- **Scan another** to check the next one.

If a code is not valid or does not match any member, a warning says so.
